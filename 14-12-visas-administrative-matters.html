12. Visas & Administrative Matters

Navigating immigration requirements and routine administrative setup is an essential part of settling into Penang. From managing Student and Dependent Passes to maintaining official documentation, handling tenancy stamping, and converting driving licenses, this chapter provides a clear roadmap for keeping your family fully compliant with Malaysian regulations.

12.1 Student & Dependant Passes

Student Pass: Mandatory for all non-Malaysian children enrolled at Stonyhurst. The school’s dedicated admissions team will guide you through the application process and submit your documents to the Malaysian Ministry of Education (MOE) and Immigration.

Dependent Pass: Issued to parents/guardians accompanying the student. Under standard Malaysian immigration guidelines, a Student Pass typically supports one primary parent/guardian on a Guardian Pass. Be sure to confirm your family’s specific visa arrangements with the school admissions office prior to arrival.

12.2 Long-Term Residency & Travel Records

MM2H (Malaysia My Second Home) / PVIP: Many expat families reside in Penang under the MM2H program, which provides a long-term, 5-to-10-year renewable social visit residency. If you hold an MM2H visa, your children can legally study at Stonyhurst, though you must secure a school enrollment stamp from the immigration department.

Travel Records & MDAC (Malaysia Digital Arrival Card) Tip: Keep digital and printed copies of your electronic arrival records (MDAC confirmation emails) alongside physical airline boarding passes. Immigration officers and visa agents frequently request proof of exact entry and exit dates during pass submissions and endorsements.

12.3 Visa Renewals

Renewal Timeline: Visa renewals are not automatic. You must submit your renewal documents to the school admissions office at least 2 to 3 months before your current pass expires.

Overstaying Penalty: Overstaying a visa in Malaysia incurs strict penalties, daily fines, administrative hassle and potential entry blacklisting. Maintain a shared calendar alert tracking passport expiry dates (passports must have 6 months’ validity) and visa pass end dates.

12.4 Essential Document Binder/ Important Documents to Keep

Maintain a secure, waterproof physical binder alongside a secure digital backup containing:

Certified true copies (CTC): Birth certificates, marriage certificates and adoption papers (officially translated into English and legalized if issued outside the Commonwealth countries).

Passport Photos: Recent passport-sized photos taken against a blue background (the official standard for Malaysian visa applications/ frequently requested for official Malaysian administrative forms).

Academic & Health Records: Official transcripts from previous schools, standardized testing results, and certified immunization/ vaccination records.

12.5 Immigration & Residency Administration

Main State Immigration Office: The primary Penang State Immigration Headquarters (Bangunan Wisma Persekutuan) is located on the mainland at Seberang Jaya. This office handles formal pass issuance, endorsements and official student pass approvals.

Urban Transformation Center (UTC Komtar, George Town): Conveniently located inside Komtar on the island for quick administrative checksum routine passport renewals (for Malaysian citizens), and general inquiries.

💡FOS Tip: Dress Code Protocol – Wear professional attire (knees and shoulders covered) when visiting any government office, as dress codes are strictly enforced.

12.6 Government & Local Administrative Services

LHDN (Inland Revenue Board/ Lembaga Hasil Dalam Negeri): Visited shortly after signing your residential tenancy agreement to have the lease legally stamped (Stamping/ Duti Setem).

JPJ (Road Transport Department/ Jabatan Pengangkutan Jalan): Handles vehicle road tax, vehicle registration transfers, and international driving permit validations or conversions (such as MM2H holder license conversions). Note that foreign students on Student Passes are not eligible for direct foreign license conversions and must obtain an International Driving Permit (IDP) or complete local licensing requirements.

12.7 Professional Guidance

💡 FOS Tip: For complex visa scenarios—such as dual-country residency, corporate director setups, or onshore pass conversions—avoid relying on informal advice. Always consult school-recommended visa specialists or certified immigration agents to manage your applications safely.
