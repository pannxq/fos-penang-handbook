19. New Family FAQ

19.1 Before Moving to Penang

Q: What documents should we prepare prior to departure?

A: Bring original and certified copies of passports, birth certificates, marriage certificates, previous school transcripts, vaccination records, eVAL student visa approvals, and any doctor letters for specialized medications.

Q: What type of clothing should we pack?

A: Penang is warm and humid year-round. Pack lightweight breathable cotton attire, swimwear, sunscreen, and formal/modest wear for visits to official government offices or places of worship.

19.2 Housing & Transportation

Q: Where do most Stonyhurst families choose to live?

A: Tanjung Tokong (such as Quayside, Andaman, or Marinox) is the top choice due to its 5-minute commute to campus. Tanjung Bungah and Gurney Drive are also popular options.

Q: How do we get around before purchasing or leasing a car?

A: Download Grab for fast, reliable e-hailing rides. For navigation, local drivers recommend using Waze for real-time traffic updates.

19.3 School & Children

Q: What are the primary school contact details and daily timings?

A: Contact Pupil Parent Services at pupilparent. services@stonyhurstpenang. edu. my or +604-2879800. Morning registration starts at 7:50 AM. If your child is absent, complete the online Attendance Form before 7:45 AM.

Q: Where can we purchase school uniforms and textbooks?

A: Uniforms are available at the School Shop beside the Chapel. Textbooks must be ordered online via Tekbookmart (www. tekbookmart. com).

19.4 Healthcare

Q: Which hospital is closest in case of medical emergencies?

A: Penang Adventist Hospital in Tanjung Tokong (+604-222 7200) is the closest emergency hospital to Stonyhurst. Gleneagles Hospital (+604-222 9111) is another premier option with comprehensive paediatric care.

Q: How do walk-in GP clinics work?

A: Local walk-in clinics (Klinik) require no prior appointment. Simply register with your passport/ID at the counter and receive doctor consultations and prescriptions on-site.

19.5 Food & Shopping

Q: Where can we buy imported groceries and Western specialty products?

A: Village Grocer at City Junction (2 minutes from school) and Jaya Grocer at Gurney Paragon offer extensive imported selections. Lotus’s Tanjung Pinang next to school is ideal for bulk everyday provisions.

Q: What online shopping platforms are most widely used in Penang?

A: Shopee and Lazada are the primary e-commerce portals. Food and quick grocery deliveries can be placed via GrabFood, GrabMart, or Foodpanda.

19.6 Banking & Payments

Q: What documents are required to open a local bank account?

A: You will need your original passport with a valid long-term visa sticker, a signed Tenancy Agreement, and an official School Verification Letter from Stonyhurst.

Q: How do e-wallets and QR payments work?

A: Most merchants accept DuitNow QR (scanned via your local banking app) and Touch 'n Go eWallet, which is also essential for street parking (via Penang Smart Parking) and toll gates.

19.7 Visas, Laws & Administration

Q: When should we initiate visa renewals?

A: Submit visa renewal paperwork to the school admissions team at least 2 to 3 months prior to expiration to prevent overstay penalties.

Q: What is the procedure if involved in a traffic accident?

A: Document the scene thoroughly, exchange details, call your insurance hotline for authorized towing, and file an official police report (Balai report) at a traffic police station within 24 hours.

19.8 Everyday Life & Common Questions

Q: Is tap water safe to drink directly?

A: Tap water should always be boiled or filtered before drinking. Most homes install filtration units (e. g. Coway or Cuckoo).

Q: How can new parents connect with the school community?

A: Join the FOS (Friends of Stonyhurst) year-group WhatsApp chats, attend FOS coffee mornings, and participate in school social events.
