2. Your First Days in Penang

The landing phase can feel overwhelming, but taking it step-by-step will help your family settle in smoothly. Here is your essential roadmap for what to prioritize before landing, upon arrival, and throughout your first few weeks on the island.

2.1 Before You Arrive

Visa & Pass Preparation: Keep printed physical copies of your student pass approval letters (eVAL) and dependent pass approvals in your carry-on luggage – do not pack these in checked luggage.

Connectivity: Pre-order a local eSIM online or activate international roaming on your home country’s SIM card for your first 48 hours.

Financial Readiness: Set-up a multi-currency digital account (such as Wise or Revolut) before departing. This allows for seamless card payments and money transfers at excellent exchange rates during your initial days.

2.2 Arriving at Penang International Airport

Penang International Airport is located in Bayan Lepas, at the southern tip of Penang Island (roughly a 30- to 45-minute drive to Tanjung Tokong/ Tanjung Bungah, depending on traffic).

Immigration: Queue at the appropriate passport control lane. If entering on a student/dependent eVAL approval, present your printed Stonyhurst acceptance letter and visa documentation to the immigration officer.

Baggage & Customs: Collect your luggage and proceed through customs. Security scanners are positioned at the exit area.

2.3 Airport Transfers & First Transportation

Grab (E-Hailing): The easiest, most reliable way to travel from the airport to your accommodation. Download the Grab app and link your payment card prior to landing. After clearing customs, order a GrabCar and follow signs to the designated e-hailing pick-up point outside the arrivals terminal.

Airport Taxis: Counter-purchased coupon taxis are available inside the arrival hall, but Grab remains the preferred choice for transparent pricing and ease when travelling with children and luggage.

2.4 Essential Apps to Install

Do yourself a favor and download these apps immediately upon arrival:

🚗 Grab: For e-hailing rides, food delivery, and quick grocery/ parcel deliveries.

💳 Touch 'n Go eWallet (TNG): Malaysia’s primary digital wallet for parking payments, convenience store purchases, toll payments and food stalls.

🗺️️ Waze or Google Maps: Essential for navigation. Local drivers overwhelmingly prefer Waze for real-time traffic updates and route changes.

💬 WhatsApp: The main communication platform in Penang, used for FOS community chats, school year groups, housing agents and local vendors.

2.5 SIM Cards & Mobile Service

A local phone number (+60) is required to register for most local apps, including Grab and Touch’n Go.

Where to Purchase: Kiosks are available inside the Penang Airport arrivals hall, or you can visit official branch stores in major malls like Gurney Plaza or Gurney Paragon.

Major Telco Providers: Maxis (Hotlink), CelcomDigi, and U Mobile. Maxis/Hotlink generally provides the most robust island-wide coverage. (Note: You must present your physical passport to register any prepaid or postpaid local SIM card.)

2.6 First 3 Days Checklist

Get Connected: Secure your local SIM card/eSIM and verify that mobile data is active.

Configure Apps: Link your card and verify registration for Grab, Waze, WhatsApp, and Touch 'n Go eWallet.

Stock Essentials: Visit a nearby grocery store (such as Lotus’Tanjung Pinang) to buy drinking water, basic provisions and snacks (note: tap water in Penang should always be boiled or filtered before drinking.)

Explore the Neighborhood: Take a walk around your temporary accommodation to locate nearby clinics, pharmacies, convenience stores and restaurants.

2.7 First Week Checklist

Visit Stonyhurst: Head to the school to complete administrative enrollment formalities, visit the school shop for uniform fitting and purchase, and collect your family security access cards.

Connect with FOS: Contact the FOS parent network to be added to your child’s official year-group WhatsApp chat.

Property Viewings: Coordinate with local real estate agents to view rental properties in key expat hubs like Tanjung Tokong or Tanjung Bungah.

Grocery Orientation: Explore primary supermarkets – such as Lotus’ Tanjung Pinang (hypermarket staple) or Jaya Grocer at Gurney Paragon (imported foods) – to locate preferred household items.

2.8 First Month Checklist

Finalize Housing: Execute your Tenancy Agreement (TA), pay the required deposits, and complete your move-in condition report.

Set Up Home Utilities: Confirm activation and transfer of household utilities:

Internet Broadband: Time Fibre is widely recognized for superior speed and reliability in Penang high-rise residences.

Electricity: Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB)

Water: Perbadanan Bekalan Air Pulau Pinang (PBA).

Open a Local Bank Account: With your signed Tenancy Agreement and a Stonyhurst school verification letter, open a local bank account (Hong Leong Bank, CIMB, Maybank, Standard Chartered Bank and HSBC are common expat choices).

Identify Medical Centers: Locate the emergency departments of nearby private hospitals – such as Penang Adventist Hospital or Gleneagles Hospital Penang – so you know your route in advance of any emergency.

At a Glance: A step-by-step landing checklist covering immediate priorities – from customs and initial transport to essential apps, housing setups, and local banking – designed to guide your family smoothly through day 1, week 1 and month 1 in Penang.
