16. School Life at Stonyhurst

16.1 Getting Started at School

Joining Stonyhurst International School Penang is supported step-by-step by the Pupil Parent Services Department.

Pupil Parent Services Support Team:

Front Desk Team: Kai Lee and Lyaa

Director of Parent Relations: LeanLee

Contact Email: pupilparent. services@stonyhurstpenang. edu. my

Reception Phone: +604-2879800

New Family Preparation Checklist:

Receive Pupil ID and assigned Line (St. Omers, Campion, Weld, or Shireburn) via email from Admissions.

Read the Junior School Handbook or Senior School Handbook.

Complete Language Selection Form (Year 3 and above).

Download and set up the AIMSpay app for meal plan subscription.

Submit the Parents/Guardian Access Card & Car Sticker Application Form.

Complete Medical and Emergency Information Form (via School Nurse).

Submit Privacy Policy PDPA, Pupil/Parent Information Update, and Media Consent forms.

Purchase uniforms, textbooks (via Tekbookmart), and required stationery.

Set up 1:1 Apple Device Programme (for Year 3 and above).

Access Cards & Car Stickers:

All parents, caregivers, and guardians must wear security access tags at all times when in school.

Car stickers must be displayed on vehicle windscreens for campus entry and security clearance.

Each family is provided with up to 2 access cards and 2 car stickers free of charge.

Additional items (up to 4 sets max per family): RM10 per extra car sticker, RM50 per extra card. Replacement fee for lost or damaged cards is RM80.

Cards and car stickers remain property of Stonyhurst International School Penang and must be returned on the child's last day of school or graduation.

16.2 School Calendar & Daily Routine

Daily Schedule:

7:45 AM: School day begins; pupils are expected to be in their Tutor Group/Classroom.

7:50 AM: Morning registration commences.

7:55 AM onwards: Late arrivals.

Drop-off & Arrival:

Smooth arrival requires leaving early to account for Straits Quay traffic during morning drop-off hours (7:30 AM – 8:00 AM).

EYFS & Year 1:

Male: Maroon Polo Shirt with Crest, Grey Shorts, White Socks.

Female: Maroon Polo Shirt with Crest, Bespoke Stonyhurst Tartan Skorts, White Socks.

Year 2 to Year 6 (Junior School):

Male: White Short-Sleeved Shirt with Crest, Grey Shorts, Easy Neck Tie (KS2+), Green Pullover (optional), White Socks, unadorned ALL black flat shoes (velcro or laced).

Senior School (Year 7 to Year 11):

Male: All White Short-Sleeved Shirt with Crest, Charcoal Trousers, Neck Tie, Green Blazer (optional), Black or Grey Socks, unadorned black leather/polishable shoes.

Female: All White Short-Sleeved Blouse with Crest, Bespoke Stonyhurst Tartan Skirt/Skorts, Neck Tie, Green Blazer (optional), White Socks, unadorned black leather/polishable shoes.

Higher Line Neck Tie, Higher Line Shirts, PE Shorts (from School Shop).

Independently sourced smart business suit with matching blazer (black trousers or knee-length skirt without revealing slits/zips), white shirts/blouses, and black leather shoes.

Line shirts corresponding to assigned Line (St. Omers, Campion, Weld, Shireburn) worn for PE and Line events. (Discounted white Line shirts remain acceptable for the 2025/26 academic year).

Rubber-soled trainers required for PE and Sports Day.

Early Years & Junior swimsuits must be plain black (available at School Shop). Black rash vests/leggings permitted. Swimming caps required.

16.4 School Meals

AIMSpay Meal Plan App:

Meal subscriptions are managed digitally via the AIMSpay mobile app. Setup is required prior to the start of term using the mother's registered email address as default.

Subscription Options & Pricing:

Termly Plan (per day): EYFS & Y1: RM17.00 | Junior: RM18.00 | Senior: RM19.50

Monthly Plan (per day): EYFS & Y1: RM17.50 | Junior: RM18.50 | Senior: RM20.00

Pay-As-You-Go (Non-Subscription): Breakfast: RM6.00 | Lunch: RM12.00

Meal Inclusions:

Subscribed plans include 1 morning snack, 1 lunch, 1 afternoon snack, daily fresh cut fruit, and dietary/menu options.

16.5 School Transport

Drop-off & Campus Access:

Driving parents must display the official school car sticker on their windscreen.

Parents dropping off younger pupils must adhere to campus safety guidelines and parking rules.

16.6 Attendance, Absence & Leave

Absence Notification:

If a pupil will be absent, late, or leaving early, parents MUST complete the online Attendance Form before 7:45 AM.

If unable to submit before 7:45 AM, call Reception (+604-2879800) immediately, followed by completing the form.

Absences are logged on iSAMS and notified to class teachers and the Principal.

Late Arrival Procedures:

Pupils arriving at or after 7:55 AM must sign in at Reception to receive a Late Pass.

EYFS & Pre-Prep 1 pupils must be escorted by a parent to the phase area glass doors (parents may not enter classrooms).

Pre-Prep 2 and above pupils make their own way independently to class with their Late Pass. Parents are strictly prohibited from entering upper school levels.

Early Dismissal Procedures:

Parents requesting early pick-up must select option 3 ("Present but leave school early") on the online Attendance Form.

Parents must report to Reception in person to sign an Exit Pass (authorized by the Senior Leadership Team - SLT).

Pupils will not be permitted to leave without an authorized Exit Pass handed to campus security guards. A parent must accompany pupils for any early departure.

16.7 Co-Curricular Activities

Co-Curricular Activities (CCAs) form an essential part of school life, helping pupils develop new skills in sports, music, drama, STEM, and languages.

Registration is conducted via the online SOCS portal in the first weeks of term.

16.8 Parent–School Communication

Primary Channels:

Pupil Parent Services: Front Desk / Reception (+604-2879800, pupilparent. services@stonyhurstpenang. edu. my).

iSAMS: Official pupil academic records, attendance logs, and school reports.

Seesaw / School Portal: Day-to-day classroom updates and learning journals.

AIMSpay: Meal subscriptions and refectory management.

Tekbookmart: Online textbook ordering.

16.9 School Events & Parent Participation

Community Events:

Orientation Days, Line competitions, school musicals, termly celebrations, and FOS coffee mornings.

Parents are encouraged to actively participate, wear access badges while on campus, and connect with the broader Stonyhurst community.
