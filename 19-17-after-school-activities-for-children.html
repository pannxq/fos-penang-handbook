17. After-School Activities for Children

17.1 Sports & Swimming

Stonyhurst International School Penang is well-known for its excellent athletic facilities, including a state-of-the-art 25m swimming pool, a large indoor sports hall, and outdoor playing fields. The school offers a wide range of sports CCAs (Co-Curricular Activities) designed for all age groups and skill levels.

Swimming: Weekly swimming sessions, swim squad training, and water safety classes.

Team Sports: Football, basketball, netball, and touch rugby.

Individual Sports: Badminton, tennis, athletics, and gymnastics.

FOS Tip: If your child is joining the Swim Squad, keep a spare dry towel and some healthy snacks in their sports bag, as swimming sessions are intensive.

17.2 Music & Performing Arts

For children who express themselves through sound and stage, the school provides exceptional music and drama CCAs.

Instrumental Music: Individual or group lessons for piano, violin, guitar, and drums.

Choral & Band: School choir, concert band, and string ensembles.

Drama & Theater: Drama clubs, annual school productions, and public speaking programs.

FOS Tip: The annual school musical is a major highlight. Auditions typically happen in Term 1, and it's a wonderful way for children to build confidence and make friends across different year groups.

17.3 Art & Creative Activities

Unleash your child's artistic potential with various hands-on creative clubs.

Fine Arts: Sketching, watercolor painting, and mixed media art.

Crafts & Design: Pottery, jewelry making, and textile design.

Media & Technology: Digital photography, school yearbook club, and videography.

17.4 Coding, Robotics & STEM

For young innovators, the STEM programs at Stonyhurst offer engaging, hands-on learning.

Robotics: Building and programming robots using LEGO Education kits.

Coding & Game Design: Scratch coding for younger children, Python and web development for older pupils.

Science Clubs: Fun kitchen chemistry, astronomy, and environmental science projects.

17.5 Language Classes

With Penang being highly multilingual, the school offers languages to enrich children's cultural understanding.

Local Languages: Beginner to advanced Bahasa Melayu and Mandarin.

Modern Foreign Languages: French, Spanish, and German clubs.

EAL Support: Additional English support clubs for non-native speakers.

17.6 Academic Support & Tutoring

For students looking to reinforce their classroom learning or stretch their academic skills.

Homework Club: A quiet, supervised space for students to complete homework before heading home.

Debate & Model United Nations (MUN): Excellent for developing critical thinking, research, and public speaking skills.

Subject-Specific Clubs: Maths challenges, science Olympiad preparation, and creative writing.

17.7 Holiday Camps & Programmes

During half-term and term-end breaks, several organizations in Penang (and occasionally the school itself) host engaging holiday camps.

Sports Camps: Multi-sports clinics, intensive swim camps at the school pool or Penang Swimming Club.

STEM & Coding Camps: 3-5 day bootcamps hosted by local providers in Tanjung Tokong and Gurney.

Creative Writing & Drama Camps: Themed workshops to keep children active and engaged during school holidays.

17.8 Choosing the Right Activity for Your Child

Balancing academics and extracurriculars is key to a happy school life.

Sign-Up Process (SOCS): Stonyhurst Penang utilizes the SOCS online portal for CCA registration. Sign-ups typically open in the first two weeks of each term. It operates on a preference-based allocation system rather than a first-come, first-served basis, so take your time to discuss options with your child.

Trial Weeks: The first week of CCAs is generally a trial week. If an activity is not a good fit, you can contact the CCA Coordinator to request a change, subject to availability.

Transportation: Remember that standard school bus services may have different schedules on CCA days (which usually run from 3:15 PM to 4:15 PM). Coordinate with your bus provider or plan for private pick-up.
