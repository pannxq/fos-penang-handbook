3. Housing & Where to Live

Finding the right neighborhood and home is one of the most important decisions you’ll make when relocating. To help you choose the best fit for your family, this section breaks down popular residential hubs, school commute factors, leasing processes, and practical home maintenance tips.

3.1 Popular Residential Areas for School Families

Most expat and international school families cluster in the northeast coastal suburbs of Penang Island for proximity to Stonyhurst, dining hubs, and daily amenities:

Tanjung Tokong (Top Choice for Stonyhurst Families): Located directly around Stonyhurst and Straits Quay Marina. High-rise developments such as Quayside, Andaman, and Marinox are exceptionally popular, offering a 5-minute commute or direct walking/ cycling access to campus.

Tanjung Bungah: A peaceful, beachside residential suburb 10–15 minutes north of the school. Offers a blend of modern oceanfront condominiums (e. g. Infinity or Springtide) and spacious landed houses, making it a favorite for families desiring extra indoor and outdoor space.

Gurney Drive & Pulai Tikus: A premium urban district 10–15 minutes south of the school. Characterized by high-end luxury condominiums, major shopping centers (Gurney Plaza and Gurney Paragon), private medical centers and a bustling city feel.

Batu Ferringhi: Located 25–35 minutes north of school, known for its resort lifestyle, beaches and night markets. While scenic, daily peak-hour commuting along the winding coastal road can be time-consuming during school runs.

3.2 Choosing the Right Neighbourhood

When choosing where to settle down, consider your lifestyle preferences:

High-Rise Condominiums: The primary choice for international families. Features include 24/7 guarded security, resort-style pools, playgrounds, gyms and professional building management. They are also easier to lock up and leave when traveling.

Landed Houses (Terraced, Semi-Detached, or Bungalows): Found primarily in Tanjung Bungah and selected areas of Tanjung Tokong (such as Seri Tanjung Pinang). Offers generous square footage, private parking, and gardens. Tenants are responsible for coordinating garden care, pest control and private security setups.

Proximity to Essentials: Ensure your preferred residence offers easy access to primary grocery hubs (such as Lotus's, Mercato or Jaya Grocer), family clinics, and walkable dining options.

3.3 School Commute & Traffic Considerations

Traffic in Penang can be highly congested during rush hours, particularly around schools and main thoroughfares.

School Peak Hours: Morning drop-off runs between 7:45 AM and 8:15 AM, and pick-up is between 3:00 PM and 4:30 PM.

Estimated Commute Times:

From Tanjung Tokong: 5-10 minutes

From Tanjung Bungah or Gurney: 15-25 minutes during peak hours

From Georgetown or Bayan Lepas: 35-50+ minutes due to major highway bottlenecks.

💡 FOS Tip: Securing a home within walking or cycling distance in Tanjung Tokong (e. g., around Straits Quay or Seri Tanjung Pinang) is highly recommended to avoid the school-run traffic stress.

3.4 Finding & Renting a Property

Finding a great rental home is straightforward if you know where to look:

Online Portals: Browse listings on PropertyGuru or iProperty to gauge market pricing, layouts and condominium facilities.

Working with Real Estate Agents: Renting is coordinated through licensed real estate agents. It is best to work with 1 or 2 responsive agents who can arrange batched viewings. (note: In Malaysia, tenants do not pay agent fees – commissions are paid directly by the landlord.)

💡 FOS Tip: Ask for agent recommendations in the FOS WhatsApp groups! Many parents work with trusted agents who specialize in Stonyhurst family requirements.

3.5 Lease Structure, Deposits & Tenancy Terms

Standard Lease Duration: 1 to 2 years, typically with an option to renew.

Standard Deposit Breakdown:

Earnest Deposit: 1 month’s rent (submitted upon offer acceptance to lock in the property; converts to the 1st month’s advance rent upon signing).

Security Deposit: 2 months' rent (fully refundable upon lease completion, subject to property condition).

Utility Deposit: 0.5 month's rent (refundable after verifying final utility settlement upon move-out).

Tenancy Agreement & Stamping Fee: A nominal administrative fee (typically RM 200–500) for agreement drafting and legal stamping with the Inland Revenue Board (LHDN).

Move-in Inventory & Condition Check: Ensure a signed inventory list is attached to the agreement. Take clear photo/video records of pre-existing condition flaws (scratches or damages) or appliance wear on move-in day.

3.6 Utilities, Internet & Home Setup

Electricity (TNB): Provided by Tenaga Nasional Berhad. Billed monthly. Air conditioning is the main driver of power bills; utilizing inverter units and ceiling fans helps optimize monthly costs.

Water (PBA): Managed by Perbadanan Bekalan Air Pulau Pinang. Water rates in Penang are very low (frequently under RM 10–20/ month).

Sewerage (IWK): Indah Water Konsortium handles wastewater services via a small flat-rate bill issued every few months.

High-Speed Broadband/ Internet: Fiber internet is widely available. TIME Internet is highly recommended for high-rise condominiums due to its superior speed and reliability (up to 1Gbps). Maxis Home Fibre and Unifi are strong alternatives.

Cooking Gas: Most residential gas stoves use standard LPG gas cylinders, which can be ordered via local neighborhood suppliers for home delivery.

3.7 Moving In & Moving Out

Management Notice: High-rise residences require notification at least 3–7 days prior to moving heavy furniture in or out.

Lift Deposits: Management offices usually collect a refundable moving/lift deposit (RM 200–500) to cover any potential common-area damage during transit.

Permitted Moving Hours: Heavy moving is strictly restricted to weekdays (9:00 AM – 5:00 PM) and Saturdays (9:00 AM – 1:00 PM). Moving is strictly prohibited on Sundays and Public Holidays in most high-rise buildings.

3.8 Common Rental Issues & Practical Tips

Bi-Annual Air-Conditioning Service: Standard tenancy agreements require tenants to service the air-conditioning units once every 6 months. Retain service receipts to present to your landlord.

Humidity & Mold Care: Penang's tropical climate means high humidity. Place disposable dehumidifiers (like Thirsty Hippo) in wardrobes and storage closets. Running your AC periodically prevents mold on leather goods and fabrics.

Ceiling/ Water Leaks: Due to high-density living, ceiling or wall moisture issues can occasionally originate from units above. Report any damp spots immediately to your landlord and condominium management for structural repair (as repairs are the landlord's legal responsibility).
