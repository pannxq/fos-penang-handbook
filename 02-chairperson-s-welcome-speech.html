Chairperson's Welcome Speech

Welcome to Our Stonyhurst Community

Dear Stonyhurst Families,

A very warm welcome to our school community! My name is Yvonne Liew, and as the Chairman of Friends of Stonyhurst (FOS) for the 2026/2027 academic year, it is my absolute pleasure to introduce this handbook to you.

When my husband, daughter and I moved from Singapore to Penang in 2024, joining Stonyhurst became our anchor. Through that transition, I quickly learned one truth: visiting Penang for a holiday and actually living here are two entirely different experiences!

Relocating and settling into a new environment—whether you are moving across the globe or simply transitioning into a new school—is an exciting journey, but it can also feel overwhelming. Navigating local nuances, finding reliable resources, and figuring out who to ask when questions arise can be daunting.

That is precisely why the 2026/2027 FOS Team designed and compiled this Parent Welcome & New to Penang Handbook.

Created by parents, for parents, this e-booklet is crafted as a practical, reassuring companion to help your family navigate both campus life and our vibrant island. Whether you are brand new to Penang or a long-standing member of our school, we hope you find something insightful and encouraging within these pages.

This handbook is designed as a living resource that will evolve alongside our growing community. If you have feedback, ideas, or topics you would love to see featured in future editions, please reach out to us anytime at .

As we embark on this academic year together, we are guided by our school motto: Quant Je Puis — "As much as I can." We look forward to connecting with you, supporting one another, and making this year a wonderful experience for our families.

Warmest regards,

Yvonne Liew

Chairman, Friends of Stonyhurst (FOS) 2026/2027

Stonyhurst International School Penang
