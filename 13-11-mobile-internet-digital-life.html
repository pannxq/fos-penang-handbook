11. Mobile, Internet & Digital Life

Malaysia boasts modern digital infrastructure with high-speed fiber internet and extensive 5G mobile coverage across Penang Island. Establishing your local phone service, setting up home Wi-Fi, and downloading a few essential apps on your first week will help your family navigate daily life effortlessly.

11.1 Malaysian Mobile Networks

Mobile networks in Penang are reliable, fast, and offer widespread 4G and 5G connectivity:

Maxis (Hotlink): Widely considered the premier mobile network in Penang, known for outstanding signal stability and top-tier data speeds in northern residential hubs like Tanjung Tokong, Seri Tanjung Pinang and Tanjung Bungah.

CelcomDigi: Malaysia’s largest merged telecommunications provider, offering broad coverage across the entire island and mainland Penang.

U Mobile: Offers highly competitive, budget-friendly prepaid and postpaid mobile data packages.

11.2 SIM Cards & eSIMs Registration

Mandatory ID Verification: Under Malaysian telecommunications law, all mobile SIM cards (prepaid or postpaid) must be registered using a valid passport or MyKad.

Where to Purchase: Physical SIM cards or instant digital eSIMs can be set up immediately upon arrival at Penang International Airport, or by visiting any network flagship store inside Gurney Plaza or Gurney Paragon.

11.3 Home Fiber Broadband

Before signing a home internet contract, check which fiber providers serve your specific condominium building or landed neighborhood:

TIME Internet: The top-rated undisputed king of high-rise home broadband. It offers blazing-fast, symmetrical speeds (up to 1Gbps) and incredible reliability.

Unifi (Telekom Malaysia) & Maxis Home Fibre: Excellent alternatives, particularly for landed houses where TIME fiber may not be laid.

Standard Contracts/ Installation: Home broadband installations typically require a 24-month service agreement/ contract. The optimal network router and Wi-Fi modem are generally provided and configured for free upon signup.

11.4 Essential Apps for Living in Penang

Download these essential apps to streamline everyday transport, navigation, and household communication:

Grab: Transport, food delivery, and parcels.

Waze: The preferred navigation tool for locals—it handles real-time traffic jams and road works better than Google Maps.

WhatsApp: The universal communication tool across Malaysia. Used for all Stonyhurst class parent chats, landlord communications, condominium management updates, local business enquiries, and FOS community chats.

11.5 Digital Grocery & Shopping Apps

Shopee & Lazada: For general e-commerce.

HappyFresh / Jaya Grocer App: For delivering fresh groceries right to your condominium lobby.

11.6 Street Parking / Navigation & Transportation Apps

Penang Smart Parking (PSP): Mandatory if you park your car on the streets of Penang. It is fully digital—simply top up with your credit card, select your zone, and pay for your parking time. No physical coupons required!

11.7 Staying Connected with School & FOS

School Communication Portal: Stonyhurst utilizes specialized apps (such as Seesaw or iSAMS) to share academic updates, school reports, and weekly parent newsletters.

FOS Class WhatsApp Directory: Be sure to connect with your designated FOS Class Parent Representative to join your child's specific year-group chat, which is invaluable for daily tips, birthday party planning, and homework questions.

At a Glance: Helps families get connected quickly and build a practical digital toolkit for communication, transport, shopping, delivery, navigation, school communication and FOS community participation.
