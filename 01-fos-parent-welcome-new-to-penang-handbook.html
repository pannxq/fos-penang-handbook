FOS Parent Welcome & New to Penang Handbook

Purpose

This handbook serves as a practical, living guide to help new Stonyhurst families transition smoothly and settle confidently into daily life in Penang. It bridges the gap between arrival and daily living by providing clear, reliable information—thoughtfully distinguishing between general Penang living, official school policies, and FOS community insights.

Editorial Approach

Designed for digital navigation, the guide is practical, family-oriented, and easy to search. To ensure clarity and accuracy, content is organized by source type:

Official School Policy: Direct, verified information regarding school rules, administrative procedures, and official guidelines (never assumed or guessed).

FOS & Community Knowledge: Vetted, practical insights and collective advice gathered from experienced school families.

Parent-to-Parent Tips: Personal recommendations and situational insights shared by fellow community members.

Note on Professional Guidance: Sections covering legal, immigration, medical, or financial matters offer practical orientation and official steps, while directing families to certified professionals for personalized advice.

Living Document Standard

To keep information reliable over time, time-sensitive details—such as school procedures, partner merchant discounts, government regulations, key contact details, operating hours, and vendor recommendations—will display a clear source tag and a Last Verified Date.
