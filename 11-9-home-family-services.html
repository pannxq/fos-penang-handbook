9. Home & Family Services

Settling into a new home in Penang involves managing everyday household logistics. This guide outlines the essential home services—from sourcing domestic help and maintaining air conditioning units to managing pest control and scheduling local transport or movers—to keep your household running smoothly.

9.1 Cleaning & Domestic Help

Many international families hire either part-time or full-time domestic helpers:

Part-Time Cleaning Services: Easily booked via mobile apps such as MaidEasy or local agencies. Rates generally run between RM 25 to RM 40 per hour (usually with a 3-hour minimum booking).

Full-Time Live-In/ Live-Out Helpers: Typically recruited through licensed employment agencies (predominantly from Indonesia, or the Philippines). Ensure all work permit applications and official immigration processes are completed to secure a legal domestic helper permit.

9.2 Babysitters & Childcare

Nannies & Babysitters: The safest and most reliable way to source trusted babysitters is through personal word-of-mouth recommendations within the FOS community chats.

Preschools & Daycare (Taska): There are several high-quality licensed daycare centers and early learning hubs located around Tanjung Bungah and Tanjung Tokong for toddlers prior to entering Stonyhurst nursery.

9.3 Air-Conditioning & Appliance Services

Due to Penang’s tropical climate and year-round humidity, air-conditioning units work hard and require routine maintenance:

Service Intervals: Most standard tenancy agreements require tenants to service all AC units once every 6 months. Failing to maintain servicing records can lead to deductions from your security deposit upon move-out.

Sourcing Technicians: Ask your landlord or condominium management office for their preferred service technician, or consult FOS parents for trusted local contacts. A basic chemical cleaning typically costs around RM 120-180 per wall unit.

9.4 Plumbing & Electrical Services

For High-Rise Condominiums: Always contact your building’s Management Office or On-site Maintenance Team first. Minor plumbing leaks or electrical trips are frequently building-wide issues that in-house maintenance can resolve quickly or refer to verified building specialists.

For Landed Properties: It is advisable to keep a trusted local handyman’s contact number handy for minor plumbing, electrical or structural fixes.

9.5 Pest Control

Penang’s tropical environment means ants, termites, and mosquitoes are common year-round:

Condominiums: High-rise residential buildings conduct routine fogging and common-area pest treatments organized by management.

Landed Homes: It is strongly recommended to sign an annual contract with a reputable pest control service (such as Rentokil) to install preventive termite barrier systems and manage garden pests.

9.6 Laundry & Dry Cleaning

Neighborhood Laundry Shops (Dobi): Available in almost all commercial blocks/shop lots. Many offer door-to-door pickup and delivery for dry-cleaning and ironing services.

24-Hour Coin Laundromats: Highly common, inexpensive, and convenient for quick washing and heat-drying bulky household items like duvets, mattress protectors or curtains.

9.7 On-Demand Moving & Transport Services

Lalamove App: The go-to app in Malaysia for instant, on-demand transport and delivery. Options range from motorcycles (for urgent documents) to cars, vans and 3-ton trucks (for moving large furniture and bulky items).

Thelorry. com: A good platform for structured, larger home-moving requirements and large-scale furniture transit.

9.8 Finding Reliable Local Service Providers

💡 FOS Tip: Avoid hiring technicians or contractors blindly from random roadside flyers or untrusted online classifieds. Always cross-reference contacts with FOS parent community chats, where families share up-to-date, tried-and-tested recommendations for reliable mechanics, handymen, and service providers.

At a Glance: A practical guide covering essential home maintenance and household management services in Penang, helping new families source reliable domestic help, maintain appliances, manage property repairs, and access verified local service providers.
